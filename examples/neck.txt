That “slight hump” on the neck is very common—people usually call it a forward head posture or a mild
dowager’s hump. The good news: if it’s from posture (not structural bone change), it’s usually reversible
with consistent work.
🧠 What’s actually happening
Your head is drifting forward (from laptop/phone use)
Upper back (thoracic spine) rounds
Neck muscles tighten in the front, weaken in the back
🏋️‍♂️ Key Exercises (do daily)
1. Chin Tucks (MOST IMPORTANT)
6
Stand against a wall
Pull your chin straight back (not down)
Hold 5 sec × 10 reps
👉 This directly fixes forward head posture.
2. Wall Angels
Back flat to wall
Raise arms like a goalpost and slide up/down
Keep lower back + head touching wall
👉 Opens chest + strengthens upper back.
3. Chest Stretch (Doorway Stretch)
6
Hands on door frame
Lean forward gently
Hold 20–30 sec
👉 Loosens tight chest pulling shoulders forward.
4. Thoracic Extensions
Use foam roller or chair
Extend upper back over it

⏱️ Timeline (realistic)

2–3 months → major correction (if consistent)

If you notice:
Hard, fixed bump (doesn’t change with posture)
Pain, numbness, or tingling
→ get checked (could be structural or fat pad)
